# Stage 6 — five-person unmoderated phone test script

**Goal:** Can someone design a shirt on their phone and get to checkout without help?

## Setup
- Phone on LTE or Wi‑Fi
- Open `https://www.lialvaro.com/design/` (or local `http://127.0.0.1:4321/design/` for staging)
- Do **not** coach mid-test; take notes

## Tasks (read aloud once, then watch)
1. Start Designing and pick **Classic Tee**.
2. Change color once.
3. Upload a photo from the camera roll (or add text that says your name).
4. Switch to **Back**, add something small, switch back to **Front**.
5. Check the IP box.
6. Tap **Export & add to cart**.
7. Open Cart and start Checkout (stop before paying if using live).

## Success criteria
- Completes steps 1–6 without asking for help
- Understands they are ordering (not requesting a quote)
- Cart shows the custom design line

- Stuck points: ___
- Would order again? Y/N
